Day 1 · 23 September

Morning · What are we?

09:00

Welcome

Goals of the symposium, logistics, and how the 1.5 days fit together — including whether our goal is a paper that defines the field.

09:10

Feedback from previous meetings

Context from the MINT proposal and its reviewer feedback — for reflection only, not to rewrite the proposal.

09:30

Infographics screening

Each participant briefly presents their “What is human mobility?” infographic; the themes carry into the day's round tables.

10:00

Individual updates

A short verbal update on what you are working on and one open question you cannot answer — no slides needed.

10:30

Coffee

11:00

Round table 1: What is mobility?

Moving from “human” mobility towards a “science” of mobility: each table proposes one candidate definition and one name for the field, then reports back.

Share your ideas on Padlet →

12:30

Lunch

Afternoon · Foundations

14:00

Round table 2: Pillars of mobility

Reshuffled tables identify the field's foundations, its canonical open problems and the expertise it requires.

Share your ideas on Padlet →

15:30

Coffee

16:00

Round table 3: New technologies & mobility

The role of AI and other emerging technologies in mobility science, and where future disruption might come from.

Share your ideas on Padlet →

17:15

Closing discussion ahead of day 2

Reflections on day 1 and what to carry into day 2.

17:30

End of day 1

19:00

Pizza and drinks (potentially Topsham Brewery)

An informal evening together — we can walk over as a group or meet there. Topsham Brewery, Maclaines Warehouse, Haven Road, Exeter EX2 8GR.

Day 2 · 24 September

Morning · Where should it go?

09:00

Open challenges

The open challenges the field must address, from the short term (~1–2 years) to the long term (~10–20 years), and the questions a proposal might ask.

10:45

Coffee

11:00

The funding landscape

Redrafting the proposal's opening around the agreed definitions and pillars, and mapping relevant funding schemes and calls.

12:00

Commitments and next steps

Next steps for the group: whether the time is right for a journal on mobility science, a possible editorial board, and when and where to meet next.

12:30

Lunch and close
