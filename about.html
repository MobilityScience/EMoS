About

Our philosophy

Why the society exists, what we value, and how we work together.

Why we exist

How people move shapes how cities grow, how diseases spread, how economies connect and how fairly opportunity is shared. Understanding mobility needs many disciplines at once: physics and computer science, geography and economics, public health and policy.

The society brings together researchers across Europe who study these questions with data, models and fieldwork. We believe the best answers come from people who usually sit in different departments, in different countries, talking to each other regularly.

How we work

We are a network, not a hierarchy. Members meet at an annual symposium, share data and methods between institutions, and start joint projects that no single group could carry alone. The map on our home page shows these collaborations as they grow.

We give particular attention to early-career researchers: shared supervision, visiting periods between member institutions, and space to present work in progress in a friendly setting.

Our values

  • Open science

    Methods, code and, where privacy allows, data are shared openly.

  • Across borders

    Collaboration between countries and institutions comes first.

  • Across disciplines

    Mobility is too complex for any single field to explain.

  • Research for society

    We aim for evidence that helps cities and policymakers act fairly.
